Detectable Tumor Types

Our system can identify the following brain tumor classifications

Glioma

A type of tumor that occurs in the brain and spinal cord, originating from glial cells.

Meningioma

A tumor that arises from the meninges, the membranes that surround the brain and spinal cord.

Pituitary

Tumors that develop in the pituitary gland at the base of the brain, affecting hormone production.

No Tumor

Healthy brain scan with no detectable tumor presence. Clean and normal MRI results.
